# Common Production Contract — V1 pilot

Language: Korean.
Primary audience: Korean small-business owners, sellers, marketers and operators relevant to the selected topic.
Goal: understand the event/problem and improve the viewer's own business judgment.

Quality constraints:
- Explain actor, event and context at first meaningful mention.
- Separate FACT / attributed CLAIM / ANALYSIS / UNKNOWN.
- Do not fabricate author's experience, customer cases or source quotes.
- Use concrete examples only when explicitly marked hypothetical unless they are in the frozen source pack.
- Keep the spoken register natural and professional.
- Do not fill duration by repetition, empty previews, repeated takeaways or rhetorical questions.
- Prefer one developing line of thought over a list of disconnected sections.
- The exact target length is run-specific and may be supplied by the user. If absent, prioritize completeness and natural pacing over a forced duration.
